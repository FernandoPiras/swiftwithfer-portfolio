## Privacy review prompts

- [ ] No real customer names  
- [ ] No personal emails (e.g. iCloud addresses)  
- [ ] No phone numbers, addresses, VAT/P.IVA of real parties  
- [ ] No real invoice amounts tied to identifiable clients  
- [ ] No identifiable health data  
- [ ] Owner-name visibility in UI explicitly approved **or** scrubbed  
- [ ] Intimate/sensitive category screens explicitly approved for public portfolio use  
- [ ] Demo/empty states preferred when they still explain the product
